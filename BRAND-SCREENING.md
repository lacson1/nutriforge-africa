# Preliminary brand screen — 17 October 2026

Requested name: SAVOURKIN. Requested compact mark: SK. Tagline: Familiar food. Your way.

Status: branding not applied. Existing preview and user data are unchanged. The requested stop-on-close-category-conflict condition was triggered by a concern about the compact SK identity, not by an exact Savourkin name match.

Checks performed:
- Exact web searches for Savourkin, Savorkin, Savour Kin and Savor Kin found no obvious exact brand match. Indexed Apple/Google app-store searches likewise found no exact match. These are incomplete index searches, not a clearance opinion.
- Companies House search for sav our kin as the single word Savourkin returned “No results found”: https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/search?q=savourkin
- Official UK IPO keyword search is linked from https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark. https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmtext/start returned 403 to the web tool and a security-check page in the browser. No trademark register search was completed; no security check was bypassed.
- Exact searches for savourkin.com, savourkin.co.uk and @savourkin found no indexed results. Domain registrations and social handle availability were not verified. Nothing was registered or purchased.

Concrete same-category concern:
- Sidekick by Sorted Food is an existing meal-planning and shopping app: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=club.sorted.cook and https://help.sortedfood.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4638361218706-What-is-Sidekick
- Its Netmums partnership document includes a yellow square SK Sidekick logo according to the retrieved PDF visual description: https://advertisements.netmums.com/wp-content/uploads/netmums-advertisements/2026/01/Netmums_MealtimesMatter-compressed.pdf . PDF screenshot retrieval failed, so that older mark was not independently inspected as pixels. The current Play listing was visually inspected: it shows a large black S on yellow with drawn accent lines. Therefore this is a specific existing-use concern requiring review, not a finding of trademark infringement or proof that all SK designs conflict.
- Numerous unrelated SK food/restaurant monograms also appeared. Their existence limits the distinctiveness of a generic two-letter approach.

Recommended next direction: retain Savourkin as a provisional full-name candidate, subject to completed trademark screening, and use an original non-initial symbol with the full wordmark rather than an SK-only app identity. Await direction before generating or applying the dependent brand package. No logo has been generated, no brand replacements applied, no production deployment, no data-key/schema changes.

## Approved continuation
The user subsequently approved Savourkin with a distinct original symbol, not SK or Savi. Applied a custom vector shared-table/food symbol and full wordmark to the local preview. The artwork was drawn directly as SVG paths; no third-party logo asset was copied. Existing storage prefixes, backup schema, package identity and URLs remain unchanged.

A follow-up search for smiling-bowl/leaf food-app logos found the motif widely used (for example https://99designs.com/inspiration/logos/bowl). The custom geometry and full name differentiate this draft from the examined Sidekick letter icon, but that does not prove visual exclusivity. No reverse-image clearance or complete image-trademark search was performed. UK IPO screening remains incomplete; domain and handle availability remain unverified. This is a provisional local brand application, not a legal clearance conclusion.

Delivered: assets/brand/savourkin-symbol.svg, savourkin-icon.svg, savourkin-logo.svg and a size-preview page. Checked 16/32/64/192 px rendering, desktop/mobile branding, both CTA journeys, existing saved week, no console errors and 74 passing regression tests. No production deployment or registration.
