# Savourkin
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Patients, families planning meals, and clinicians supporting clients, as confirmed in the conversation.
## Product Purpose
Browse familiar foods, build plates, save a week of meals and produce a household shopping list.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing static HTML/CSS/JavaScript application. Browser-local storage and backups must remain compatible. No production deployment authorised for this preview. Preserve nutrition audit safeguards: 60 source-matched original records, 78 distinct variants, 370 excluded originals. Unknown nutrition is incomplete, never zero or silently substituted. Clinical tools remain withdrawn.
## Brand Commitments
Savourkin name, original shared-table symbol and “Familiar food. Your way.” Reference HTML supplies cocoa, gold and cream visual direction; the user wants smaller ingredient thumbnails leading to a larger plate. Actual tasks remain readable and static.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable controls/dialogs, focus restoration, reduced motion, large touch targets and flowing mobile layouts.
