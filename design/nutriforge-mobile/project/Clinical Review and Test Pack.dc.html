PART 2

Patient test, 5 people × 30 minutes

Who to recruitUK adults of West African or Caribbean heritage with type 2 diabetes and/or high blood pressure. At least 2 aged over 60, 1 person with low confidence using phones, and 1 who is not a first-language English speaker.
SetupUse a real iPhone, or a phone-sized screen. Start from onboarding at 2a, then use 3a and tabs 4a–4d. Ask them to think aloud. Don't help unless they're stuck for 60 seconds.
The home-screen decisionTask 2 settles it. If 3 or more of the 5 go straight to search, make "Check" the home screen. Otherwise keep "Today".

After the tasks, ask 1. What did the dots next to each food mean to you? 2. Was there a food you cook that we didn't have? 3. Would you show the handout to your GP? Why, or why not? 4. Did anything make you worried about your medicines? 5. Would you open this tomorrow? What would make you open it?
