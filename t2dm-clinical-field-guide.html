Food guidance, with evidence in view.

Clinical tools are unavailable

The old food tiers, glycaemic estimates, glucose curves and treatment claims have been withdrawn. They do not provide reliable personalised medical guidance. Saved meals remain in your main-app planner.

A practical starting point

Use the food list to explore source-labelled ingredients and portions. Discuss changes to diabetes meal timing and portions with your care team.

Insulin and some diabetes medicines can cause low blood glucose; skipping or delaying meals can increase that risk. This app does not calculate medication doses.
